<img src="media/image1.jpeg" style="width:3.73819in;height:3.67917in" />To
celebrate the 25<sup>th</sup> anniversary of the Green Album, I have
compiled this set which contains the album in its entirety as well as
B-Sides, Demos (whether full-band or just Rivers Cuomo), and plenty of
live material. You may notice differences in the remasters, and that is
because to achieve the more dynamic sound as well as making a more
interesting listening experience, I opted to base the remaster off the
2001 leak of the album. This has a lot of changes, some insignificant,
others much more so, but the leak was unmastered, meaning the
compression added to the final album is nonexistent in that version.
