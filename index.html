weezer 25

To celebrate the 25th anniversary of the Green Album, I have compiled this set which contains the album in its entirety as well as B-Sides, Demos (whether full-band or just Rivers Cuomo), and plenty of live material. You may notice differences in the remasters, and that is because to achieve the more dynamic sound as well as making a more interesting listening experience, I opted to base the remaster off the 2001 leak of the album. This has a lot of changes, some insignificant, others much more so, but the leak was unmastered, meaning the compression added to the final album is nonexistent in that version.
